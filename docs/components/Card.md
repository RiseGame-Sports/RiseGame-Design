# Card

The default container: 8px radius, 1px hairline border (#D6D6D6 on light, #2E2E2E on dark), whisper-thin shadow. Flat, not floating.

Notes
- Cards are white on the paper #F2F2F2 page; the shadow is the `shadow-card` token at full strength.
- dark tone for black chrome; hover border goes accent there, ink on light.
- No colored left-border accents, no gradient fills.
